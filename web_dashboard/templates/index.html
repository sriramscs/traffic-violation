🎯 Why Vision Transformer (ViT)?

Global patch self-attention allows the system to analyze subtle features (e.g. seatbelt sash contrast across dark clothing or helmet curvature under heavy glare) where local CNN kernels fail.

🔄 Why ByteTrack?

Temporal Kalman filtering and low-score detection association prevent duplicate penalty ticketing and eliminate false alarms caused by temporary camera occlusion.

🔍 Why Grad-CAM++?

Provides mathematical accountability and Explainable AI (XAI) verification, highlighting the exact image region responsible for flagging the citation.
